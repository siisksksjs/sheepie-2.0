# Sheepie blog illustration style (applies to every image)

Look: contemporary editorial vector illustration, like the spot illustrations in a premium magazine or a modern sleep-brand blog.
- Crisp flat vector shapes with clean edges. SOLID colour fills only.
- NO gradients, NO paper grain, NO noise texture, NO watercolour, NO airbrush, NO soft glow, NO 3D render, NO photorealism, NO anime.
- People are simplified and stylised: geometric forms, simple closed-eye lines, minimal facial detail, no blush gradients, no glossy hair strands. Calm, adult, Indonesian.
- Shading only as a few flat darker shapes (one shade per colour). Bold silhouettes, big colour fields, strong simple composition.
- Palette, strictly these and nothing else:
  - deep navy #22336a (night, background, hair, shadows)
  - mid navy #3a4f8f (secondary shadows)
  - sky blue #a3c1e0 (product, bedding)
  - pale sky #dbe7f3 (highlights)
  - cream #fbf8f1 (skin highlights, sheets, light)
  - one small soft-peach #f6cdb8 accent only (a lamp glow, a mug)
  - skin: warm tan #d9a77f with one flat shadow #b9845e
- Signature motif: Indonesian Mega Mendung batik clouds (see the attached mendung reference) as flat sky-blue line shapes, used to show air, sound, light or support. Keep them simple and few.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no UI.
- Landscape 3:2 composition (1536×1024). The subject sits in the centre-right; keep the left 15% calm (plain background) because a title card sits next to it.
